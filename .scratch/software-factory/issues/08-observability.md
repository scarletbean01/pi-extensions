# Define factory operation and visibility

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Assignee: deplague (Pi session 01a1f470-b9ec-7777-8514-87f2a4f9b629)
Blocked by: 01, 02, 03, 05, 06, 07

## Question

What must a person see and control to operate the factory and its runs in Pi/Herdr, including pending inputs, human requests, failures, cancellation, stopping and recovery? Define the user-facing operational outcomes and notification needs without assuming a web interface. Include queue order and how to distinguish ordinary capacity waits, a checkout held by another factory, and an uncertain run that requires inspection, as decided in [Decide concurrency and side-effect isolation](05-concurrency-isolation.md).

Under [Define factory definition changes](06-versioning.md), determine how operators distinguish the current version from the version bound to an input or run, see which settings inherit Pi's defaults, and inspect publication validation errors and version-related queue blocks.

Under [Define durability and recovery guarantees](07-recovery.md), decide how status exposes the evidence for a safe launch retry versus an uncertain capacity hold, and how operators resume or take over, retry a proven unreleased launch, or record clearance. Distinguish a recorded intervention response from consumption, verdict and successor admission.

## Answer

- **Pi/Herdr views and alerts.** Show a compact live summary in the managing Pi session: management state, active run, queue and what needs attention. Provide on-demand views for queue entries and receipts, intervention requests, runs and recovery evidence. Reuse Bootstrap's run inspection and live graph views where practical. Prominently surface new intervention requests, failed runs, blocked queue heads and uncertain holds in the managing session without notifying on every ordinary transition. The underlying records, not the alerts, are authoritative; missed alerts do not hide outstanding items, including after takeover.
- **Queue and receipts.** List queued inputs in durable acceptance order. Show why the head cannot launch: factory stopped or unmanaged, its own occupied slot, checkout held by another factory, bound-version or compatibility block, or an uncertain hold. Identify the blocking factory, input or run when known. Show later entries as waiting behind the head rather than silently skipping it. Read-only lookup by submission reference shows the original input ID, bound definition version and current disposition; acceptance does not imply launch.
- **Definition visibility.** Show the current version separately from the bound version on the receipt, queue entry, run and report. Distinguish frozen settings from policies that inherit Pi's available tools or skills at launch. Publication failures identify the node, field or unmet prerequisite where known and show that the current version did not change. A compatibility block identifies the input's bound version and unmet prerequisite; it is not an offer to substitute a newer version.
- **Intervention and routing.** Show the question, reason, evidence or artifact references, node and attempt, plan-defined choices and any recommendation. Allow optional guidance alongside a choice or explicit decline. Keep a revision choice selectable when its budget is exhausted and warn of the recorded-plan consequence before selection. Distinguish pending, resolved, cancelled and uncertain requests. Show response recorded, response consumed, verdict recorded and successor or revision admitted as separate facts; mark a stage unknown or inapplicable where warranted. Recording a response is not proof that work resumed.
- **Authority and controls.** Other Pi sessions may inspect without managing. Factory mutations require the managing session; explicit resume or takeover must establish exclusive authority before changes are allowed. Provide direct Pi commands or interactive selections for people, and structured tools for agent assistance, with the same eligibility checks. Discard, cancel, takeover, launch retry and operator clearance require explicit human confirmation of the target and consequences; the agent may prepare the action but cannot confirm it on the operator's behalf. Exact command names and presentation belong in the build specification.
- **Recovery evidence and distinct effects.** Show the evidence that a launch never released and cannot release, or why execution or capacity release remains uncertain and what evidence is missing. Offer explicit launch retry only when eligible; an uncertain attempt must not be replayed. Clearance requires the reason and evidence established by the recovery decision, with an explicit acknowledgement that it releases the hold without resolving or undoing past effects. Keep stopping admission and launches, cancelling a run, closing tabs and clearing a capacity hold separate; none implies another. Show cancellation intent separately from settled execution or released capacity.
